## Implemented scope

The dormant owner consumes this context's actual BootPlanSummary,
RepairedBootPairs and CheckedBootOpen chain. It retains the private journal,
original source-backed material and the loaded schema election. A placement
checks the exact prefix, source coverage, ordered schemas, filed instruction,
turn and original Decision bound. The reserved MeasureDelta supplies actual
counts and raw evidence. Checked head, tail, generation and terminal fit plus
Recorded placement produce one private generation grant. Prospective IDs burn
once but do not become resident before a matching terminal confirms them.

The sole common pump owns the decode port. It checks request completion,
reservation identity, original bounds and observed cancellation before mutation.
Generated independently checks input/output/terminal counts and token domains,
residency and capacity, then retains the physical fact and owed model events
before fallible mark hooks. Cancel drains its separate AtRest leg. Installed
journal ownership rejects unreserved Open, Append, Flush, Elide and ReFeed.
Read-only sort measurement provides no placement or generation capability.

Required summary traffic holds only a wholly unsent generation write. The same
decoder remains visible to the sole pump for receive, terminal readiness and
standing failure. The scoped hold restores before fallible continuation. A
same-pump Stop suppresses first-byte write while retaining decoder health.
Started segmented sends and Cancel still progress. This addresses #267 Planner
review 5624358977 row 2 without changing lifecycle or cancellation policy.

Native marks derive from actual task/effect filing receipts and observed
generations. Prompt positions require the complete original Text interval.
StepOutcome itself supplies no native endpoint. Refreshed PlanSummary receipt
handoff precedes further placement. Already admitted native scope proposals
remain held through Failed-phase terminal accounting.

Cut queues until the actual prior turn and admitted input/task owners yield.
Only then does its one Cut clock start. It freezes the requested boundary,
drains existing filing/read/native obligations, measures rebuild plus tail,
then confirms exact physical Flush/Elide counts before private Staged transfer.
An already owed effect summary retains both original Decision and Cut bounds.
The summary's private read uses their minimum. A standalone summary-owned Cut
remains distinct. Neither exception admits a new ordinary read or workflow.

Pending Text retains its actual source lease through filing or correct refusal.
Staged keeps ordered rebuild/carry and exclusive ownership. Its next append
remeasures the full delta without duplicating carry or old message IDs. Empty
Elide sends and records no elision. Local fit/address refusals record, close an
actual turn and retain their response before another operation is admitted.

An extracted BootPlanSummary still cannot install placement after the actual
boot owner observes Stop, Leave or JoinLeave. The exact proof returns unchanged
for the pending failed-Enter composition; taking the envelope cannot restore it.
Observe and no-command controls still install.

Stop revokes the matching grant and current-turn admission until its actual
TurnClosed records. Leave retains the run-wide fence. Failed/Releasing owners
retain exact raw exchange, author ticket and Recorded sequence, drain under the
earliest applicable original/shutdown bound and report incomplete explicitly.
Trace-only accounting grants no placement, generation or successful Staged.
